Connect Your Free Database

Follow these 4 quick steps to enable unlimited, secure cross-device document sync between your laptop, iPad, and team members:

  1. Create a Firebase Account: Go to Firebase Console (log in with any Google Account) and click "Add Project". Name it PUEO-Wireless.
  2. Create Realtime Database: Inside your project dashboard, click "Realtime Database" in the left sidebar menu, then click "Create Database". Choose your location and click Next.
  3. Set Rules to Public: In the Realtime Database panel, switch to the "Rules" tab. Change both ".read" and ".write" values to true, then click "Publish".

    Note: This allows password-free sync. For high-security setups, you can configure standard Firebase auth tokens later.

  4. Copy and Paste URL: Copy the database URL displayed at the top of your database window (it looks like https://your-app-default-rtdb.firebaseio.com/). Open your saved index.html in a text editor, find const dbUrl = "REPLACE_ME_WITH_YOUR_FIREBASE_URL"; near line 210, and paste your URL there!

Current Mode: Local Sandbox Mode. Your documents will save in your current web browser but won't synchronize across other computers or iPads until you update the database link in the code.
